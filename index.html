Questions

Good to know

Is it free?

Yes. Every feature is free.

Where is my data stored?

What you create stays in this browser on this device. You can export a backup or delete everything from Settings.

Do I need an API key?

Not to try it: the app runs in demo mode with sample AI output. For live AI, connect a free Groq or OpenRouter key in Settings.

What can I type into the AI box?

Anything you would ask a helpful colleague who knows the app, such as: "I just bought 6 eggs, a bag of spinach and 500g of chicken". It plans the steps, runs the app's own functions and tells you what it did. The Instructions tab saves preferences every AI feature follows.

Can I see the code?

Yes. It is open source under the MIT license on GitHub, with a public test suite.
